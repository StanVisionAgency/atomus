Marks anything an AI wrote or changed. With an explanation it opens a popover that says why the person sees this and which model made it. Once the person edits the content, it reads "Edited" and offers "Revert to AI".

## Usage

```tsx
import { AILabel } from '@stanvision/atomus-react';

<AILabel
  label="AI generated"
  explanation={<p>Written from the 14 comments on this pull request.</p>}
  model="Atomus Pro"
  edited={text !== generated}
  onRevert={() => setText(generated)}
/>
```

### Props at a glance

Full types and defaults are in [React API](#react-api) at the end of the page.

- `label`, `size` (`xs` · `sm` · `md`), `variant` (`chip` · `inline` · `icon`).
- `explanation`, `explanationTitle` and `model` fill the popover.
- `edited` switches to "Edited"; `onRevert` adds "Revert to AI".

## Date picker

Calendar dropdown: single date or range.

Variants: 2

| Property | Type / options |
|---|---|
| Type | Single · Range |

**Do**

- Show today and the selected range clearly.
- Let people type the date in Date input.
- Offer presets for common ranges.

**Forbidden**

- Never hide the year when a range crosses years.
- Never disable past dates unless the task requires it.
- Never make people click through a calendar for birth dates — use Date input.
